Creator

My name is Eryck Borges, I’m 18 years old, and I developed this project out of my love for the Pokémon franchise. It all started as a study project on Dio.me, where I learned many new things that helped me grow as a developer. I hope you’re enjoying the app and that it’s truly useful for your gameplay. The main goal has always been to make your journey easier: helping you navigate the map, showing details about each Pokémon, and even redirecting you to the official Nintendo or Game Freak pages if you want to purchase a specific game. That’s it! I’ll wrap this up by sending you a big hug from another passionate Pokémon fan.
